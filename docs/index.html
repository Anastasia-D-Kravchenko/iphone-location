Put your iPhone and Apple Watch anywhere on the map.

An open-source app and Mac server that move the location of your own devices using Apple's developer services — from a Mac, or with no Mac at all, even on mobile data.

Three ways to move

Mac

Mac server

A small Python server and web page that call devicectl. iPhone and Watch, over cable or home Wi‑Fi. The iPhone app works as a remote.

No Mac

This iPhone

The app pairs with its own iPhone and opens a developer tunnel to itself through LocalDevVPN. Works away from home, on 5G.

No Mac

iPhone + Watch + AirPods

The app reaches the Watch over Bluetooth like a Mac does and moves both together. AirPods follow the iPhone they're connected to.

Made for everyday testing

Hold timer

Pick 1 minute to 4 hours. The real location comes back by itself, even if the app is closed.

Prolong or stop

A notification a minute before the end: keep the fake 15 more minutes, or go back now.

Free Apple ID

Builds with a free account; a login agent re-signs and reinstalls the app before the 7 days run out.

How the Watch part works

  1. Bluetooth. Connect to the Watch and open an L2CAP channel (PSM 0x85) — the RemotePairing control channel.
  2. Pair-verify with the pairing made once from the Watch's Developer Mode screen, then ask for a tunnel listener.
  3. Bonjour. The Watch advertises _rp-tunnel._tcp; connect over Wi‑Fi or AWDL with Network.framework.
  4. Tunnel. TLS-PSK → CDTunnel → RemoteServiceDiscovery, via the idevice library.
  5. Set location with CoreDevice's location service — it stays after disconnecting. Done in about 3 seconds.

Every step was found by watching what a Mac does when Xcode talks to a Watch, then doing the same from an iPhone app. Dead ends and measurements are documented too: the Watch closes tunnels after ~8 s, location simulation over Instruments reverts on disconnect, and a Watch next to its iPhone takes the iPhone's location — so both are moved together.

Get started

git clone https://github.com/Anastasia-D-Kravchenko/iphone-location.git
cd iphone-location/ios
cp local.env.example local.env
rustup target add aarch64-apple-ios
Vendor/build_idevice.sh
./build_install.sh
You need
iPhoneiOS 27, Developer Mode on
Apple WatchwatchOS 27, Developer Mode on (optional)
MacXcode 26+, XcodeGen, Rust — to build once
No-Mac iPhone modeLocalDevVPN

Then follow the setup guide: trusting the developer profile, pairing the iPhone and the Watch, and the optional Mac server.

Use it responsibly. Only on devices you own, for development and testing. Many apps — games, banking, dating, workplace check-ins — forbid fake locations and may ban accounts. Don't use it to mislead people.
